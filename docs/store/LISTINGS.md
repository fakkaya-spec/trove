## 🇬🇧 English (birincil)

**App name (30):** `BoatCheck: Boat Inspection`
**iOS subtitle (30):** `Charter check-in & provisions`
**Play short (80):** `Inspect charter boats, prepare your own boat and provision the crew — offline.`
**iOS keywords (100):** `yacht,charter,boat,sailing,checklist,inspection,check-in,bareboat,provisioning,skipper,catamaran`

**Full description:**

⚓ One app to inspect the boat, prepare the voyage and provision the crew.

BoatCheck is the digital yacht handover and inspection companion for charter guests, boat owners and skippers. Built for real conditions: bright sun, one free hand, no internet.

CHARTER CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT
• Exception-based inspection: confirm a clean section in one tap, document only the problems
• Photograph existing damage, meters and equipment — every record is time-stamped
• Fuel, water and engine-hour readings as first-class data
• Check-out shows check-in photos side by side for guided re-shooting
• Share a factual handover summary — facts and evidence, no accusations

PREPARE YOUR OWN BOAT
• Pre-departure checklist: weather, engine (WOBBLE), safety gear, crew briefing, documents
• Return & secure checklist so the boat is left right, every time
• Critical items can never be skipped silently

PROVISION THE CREW
• Smart shopping list from your crew size, trip length, climate and cooking plans
• Every quantity is an editable estimate — tap ⓘ to see exactly how it was calculated
• Enter what's already on board; buy only what's missing
• Purchased / packed states, custom items, share via any app

BUILT FOR THE DOCK
• Works fully offline — everything is saved instantly on your device
• Large touch targets, dark mode, one-thumb use
• 5 languages: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Русский, Español
• No account required

BoatCheck records facts and evidence. It does not make legal, insurance or seaworthiness decisions, and provisioning quantities are planning estimates, not guarantees. Local legal requirements may differ.
